Privacy policy

Applies to the HSK 4 Prep website (hsk.arnayem.top) and its Android app. Last updated 24 September 2026.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, the name you choose, and your password stored only as a secure hash (never in readable form).
  • Your practice history: answers, scores, word mastery, flashcard schedule, mistakes, mock-exam results and your app settings.
  • Leaderboard: your display name (you can change it) and, for each day, how many answers you gave and how many were correct. Other signed-in users can see your display name and points on the daily and weekly leaderboards — never your email. You can hide yourself under Settings → Leaderboard; your points keep counting privately and reappear if you turn it back on.
  • Sign-in cookie: one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs: standard web server logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser type), used only to keep the service running and secure.

How it is used

  • Only to run the app: sign you in, save and restore your progress on any device, and send the 6-digit codes for email verification and password reset.
  • No ads, no analytics services, no selling or sharing of your data.

Where it is kept

  • On the site's own server (Vultr, United States).
  • Verification and reset emails are sent through Google's Gmail service.
  • Backups: a nightly copy of the account database is kept on the server for 14 days, and a weekly copy is emailed to the site owner's private mailbox.
  • Listening audio is produced by the voice engine on your own device; the text is not sent anywhere else.

Deleting your account

  • In the app or website: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all practice history from the live database immediately.
  • Copies inside existing backups disappear as those backups expire (14 days on the server; emailed weekly copies until the owner deletes them).
  • Accounts that are never email-verified are deleted automatically after 7 days.

Children

The service is meant for people preparing for the HSK exam and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact the site owner through arnayem.top.
